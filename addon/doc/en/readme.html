NewsHub for NVDA

  • Author: Aris Yohanes Elean, S.Kom
  • Version: 1.2.1
  • Supported NVDA Versions: 2022.1.0 – 2026.1.0

Overview

Reading news from various national and international news portals such as kompas.com, detik.com, and others is truly enjoyable. Aside from providing reliable information, it also broadens our knowledge as readers. However, as we know, the web pages of these news portals are not friendly to screen readers. There are many advertisements and complex navigation elements that make reading difficult. That is why "NewsHub For NVDA" is here!

With this add-on, you can read news from trusted news portals easily and comfortably. In version 1.2.1, the add-on supports 5 major Indonesian news portals across 76 official rubrics:

  • Kompas.com (23 Rubrics): News, Nasional, Global, Megapolitan, Regional, Pemilu, IKN, Bola, Tekno, Otomotif, Entertainment, Sains, Health, Money, Tren, Properti, Lifestyle, Hype, Travel, Homey, Food, UMKM, Edukasi.
  • Detik.com (27 Rubrics): Detik news, Detik finance, DetikInet, DetikHot, DetikSport, SepakBola, DetikOto, DetikTravel, DetikFood, Detik Health, Wolipop, DetikX, 20Detik, DetikFoto, DetikEdu, DetikHikmah, DetikProperti, DetikPop, DetikJateng, DetikJatim, DetikJabar, DetikSulsel, DetikSumut, DetikBali, DetikSumbagsel, DetikJogja, DetikKalimantan.
  • CNN Indonesia (8 Rubrics): Nasional, Internasional, Ekonomi, Olahraga, Teknologi, Otomotif, Hiburan, Gaya hidup.
  • CNBC Indonesia (7 Rubrics): Market, My Money, News, Tech, LifeStyle, Sharia, Entrepreneur.
  • Tempo.co (11 Rubrics): Permainan baru, Politik, Hukum, Ekonomi, Lingkungan, Wawancara, Investigasi, Cek fakta, Tokoh, Newsletter, Info tempo.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
NVDA+Shift+K Open the NewsHub for NVDA dashboard from anywhere
Tab / Shift+Tab Move focus across dashboard controls in the 12-step focus order
Up / Down Arrow Select a news site, rubric, or article title, or read article content line by line
Alt+[ Go to the previous article in the active rubric list
Alt+] Go to the next article in the active rubric list
F5 Refresh the article list for the active rubric
Ctrl+R Reload the full content of the currently selected article
Alt+R Return focus to the rubric selection control
Alt+S Return focus to the news site selection control
Escape Contextually return to the previous stage or close the dashboard
